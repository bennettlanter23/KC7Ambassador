Your Path

Five stages, from applicant to campus lead.

Every ambassador moves through the same journey. Here's what each stage looks like, what's expected of you, and what you earn along the way.

Why Become an Ambassador?

Why You Should Apply

  • Meet professionals in the cybersecurity field.
  • Showcase your individual strengths.
  • Teach your knowledge to peers and other students.
  • Create something authentically your own.

What You'll Do

  • Build mini workshops for 7th–12th grade students, playing to your strengths.
  • Pilot your workshop, then lead it at schools of your choice.
  • Help students learn to navigate tools like KC7 independently.
  • Represent the Cybersecurity program at The HILL.
What Success Looks Like

Have fun and make this hyper-specific to you.

  • Use KC7 and Student Ambassador resources.
  • Create a workshop that you like — not one built for someone else.
  • Pilot the workshop with your peers — play test it.
  • Facilitate the workshop at 3 different schools of your choosing.
  • Send feedback forms to administrative staff at your 3 schools of choice.
  • Present your project at STLP with your team.
The Journey
Stage 1

Apply

Submit the Student Ambassador application with a short note on why you want to bring KC7 to your school. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.

~10 minutes Open to any current student
Stage 2

Onboard

Accepted ambassadors join the onboarding call, get access to the Ambassador Discord/Slack, and set up their KC7 classroom for their school.

1 onboarding call Classroom created Earns: Rookie Ambassador badge
Stage 3

Train

Work through the Ambassador training path — running your first Daily7 walkthrough, presenting a game to a group, and learning the toolkit.

Self-paced Training checklist Earns: Certified Ambassador badge
Stage 4

Activate

Host your first live event — a watch party, workshop, or club presentation — and recruit your first cohort of classmates into KC7.

1+ event hosted 5+ classmates recruited Earns: Active Ambassador badge + swag
Stage 5

Lead

Take on a full term as campus lead — running recurring events, mentoring new ambassadors at your school, and giving feedback that shapes the program.

Full term completed Earns: Campus Lead badge Alumni network eligibility
