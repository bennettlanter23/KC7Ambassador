What is a Student Ambassador?

KC7 Student Ambassadors are students who bring cybersecurity education to their own school — introducing classmates to Daily7 challenges, running Blue Team workshops, and helping build the next generation of defenders. In return, ambassadors get early access, recognition, and support directly from the KC7 team.

Core Responsibilities

  • Host a Daily7 watch party — a weekly or monthly meetup where classmates solve the day's challenge together.
  • Run a workshop or CTF — introduce a game path or blue-team scenario to a club, class, or event.
  • Recruit classmates — help new players create accounts and join your school's classroom.
  • Share your progress — post streaks, badges, and leaderboard wins to keep momentum going.
  • Represent KC7 — at career fairs, club days, and cybersecurity events on campus.

Perks & Benefits

  • Exclusive Ambassador badge and recognition on the KC7 leaderboard.
  • KC7 swag shipped for your first event.
  • Letter of recommendation / resume credit for completed terms.
  • Early access to new game paths and features before public release.
  • Direct line to the KC7 team for questions, feedback, and event support.
